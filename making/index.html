Making Mir

Mir is a Korean AI actress built entirely in Higgsfield. Her series runs on Instagram @mir_actionai. This page shows the work behind it, including the failures.

Part 1 · 26s

I rebuilt her face 5 times

  • V1: photo references only. She aged in new scenes.
  • V2: full character sheet. Still looked "AI".
  • V3: Soul ID on 5 face photos. Face locked, body off.
  • V4: added 6 full-body photos. Body fixed.
  • V5: added the expressions she plays (resolve, shout, pain).
  • The lock: 1 face Element plus 1 Element per outfit.
Part 2 · 22s

3 rules that stop face drift

  • Photo refs only. One drawing in the refs drags her into anime.
  • Real person first, doll second. Too much "doll-like" gives a different girl.
  • Every outfit is its own Element. Spell the detail: torn at the knees, down to the toes.

Pipeline

Soul 2.0 (5 retrains) → face and outfit Elements → Nano Banana Pro keyframes → Seedance 2.5 / Kling shots → edit, music and subtitles. Action blocking is pre-visualised in Blender before any generation.
