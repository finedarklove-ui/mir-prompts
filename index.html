2Make 4 outfit swaps

Put image 1 as the first reference and use the prompt below: the composition stays, only the outfit and hands change. Keep pose changes tiny — that is what makes the video feel like only the clothes flip when the blade passes.

315-second video with Seedance 2.5

In Seedance 2.5 choose omni reference and add the 5 images in order. The trick is writing exact timings: one blade pass every 3 seconds, one outfit change per pass.

Mode omni referenceLength 15sAudio off (add music later)
Video prompt
Photorealistic 15-second vertical 9:16 fashion lookbook video, ceiling-fan POV, locked top-down camera that never moves. Same composition as all five reference images: steel ceiling fan at the top, dusty-blue carpet, pink round cushion under her head, the same young woman lying in the same place. Keep her face, hair and body identical.
FAN: the camera is mounted just under the fan, so the wooden blades are very close to the lens and look huge. The blades rotate slowly and continuously at one constant speed the whole time, never stopping. Exactly one blade sweeps across her every 3 seconds (at 3s, 6s, 9s, 12s) and briefly covers her body.
OUTFIT CHANGES: exactly one change per blade pass, only at the moment the blade covers her; when it moves on she is revealed in the next outfit.
0-3s: image 1. 3s -> image 2. 6s -> image 3. 9s -> image 4. 12s -> image 5 to the end.
Her eyes are always warm and smiling, looking up. Only tiny natural movements between passes. Soft daylight, real fabric, real skin. No text.

4Tips to avoid failed generations

  • Lock the composition in one image. For images 2–5, always use image 1 as the first reference and start with "Keep EXACTLY the same photo".
  • Blades stop and start? Always include "constant speed, never stopping". Without it the blades only whip around at each outfit change.
  • Outfit changes without a blade? Write the timing in numbers, like "every 3 seconds". 5 outfits fit 15 seconds perfectly.
  • Expression: if the eyes look off, remake just that outfit image with "soft smiling eyes". The video copies the photo’s expression.
  • Draft first: check the flow at 480p and only go 1080p when you love it — it saves credits.
